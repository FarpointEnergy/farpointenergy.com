Utilities

Substation, auto and distribution transformers built to your standard specification.

Utility purchases run on a standard specification and an approval cycle. The quotation answers your specification clause by clause; outline, nameplate, control schematics, CT curves and bushing drawings are submitted as applicable, with review time built into the schedule. Taps, impedance and grounding are set for the system and loading requirements.

Applications

Utility-scale solar

Main interconnection and medium-voltage transformer packages for solar projects.

Solar output varies with irradiance, site conditions and the plant operating limits. For main interconnection transformers, loading and power factor guide the MVA rating and cooling stages; natural and forced cooling are selected for that duty. Medium-voltage transformer packages connect inverter blocks to the collector system, with voltage, impedance, grounding and harmonic requirements matched to the equipment and system studies.

Applications

Battery storage

Main power and medium-voltage transformers for standalone and hybrid storage.

Storage transformers carry charging and discharging duty, which can involve repeated periods at high load. The operating cycle, ambient conditions and inverter harmonics inform thermal margin, conductor design and cooling. Both main interconnection transformers and medium-voltage equipment packages are specified for that duty; monitoring can be added so the operator can follow the unit's condition from the control room.

Applications

Hybrid solar + storage

Main interconnection and medium-voltage packages for generating and charging.

Solar and a battery may share a point of interconnection, with separate transformer packages for their equipment blocks. Export limits, simultaneous operating cases and grid charging determine the duty of the main transformer and each medium-voltage package. Both generating and charging cases go into the design, and unit quantities and delivery phases can follow the project build-out.

Applications

Data centers

Incoming substation and downstream distribution transformers for phased campus delivery.

A data-center campus can combine sustained demand with changing loads as equipment and halls come online. Incoming substation transformers serve the utility connection; downstream distribution transformers supply the voltage levels required across the site. Ratings, quantities and delivery phases follow the electrical design and construction sequence. On-load tap changers, sound limits and monitoring are included where the package calls for them.

Wind

Collector step-up transformers for onshore wind projects.

Wind output arrives in long, variable runs with periods at full load. The collector transformer is rated for sustained output at the collector voltage, with the impedance and low-side grounding set to the collector system study and the interconnecting utility's requirements.
