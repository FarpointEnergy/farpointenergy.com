Privacy

Inquiry information

How information sent through this website is handled.

Information you send

The quotation form collects your contact details and the project information you choose to provide. Farpoint uses it to review your requirement, prepare a response and communicate about the potential supply.

Form and email processing

The form is submitted through FormSubmit and routed to quotes@farpointenergy.com. Email attachments go directly to that business inbox. FormSubmit and the email provider process information as part of those services. Contact us first if a project requires a separate confidential document process.

Website services

This static website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses Google Fonts. Those services receive technical request information when your browser loads their resources. Farpoint uses first-party website analytics to understand visits, product interest, quotation steps and technical errors. Random browser and session identifiers use browser storage; permitted campaign codes and broad referring-source categories describe how a visit arrived. The tracker does not collect typed form contents, names, email addresses, specification text or attachments. Technical request records include available IP metadata; network information may indicate an organization but does not identify a person or establish their employer. Collection is processed through Cloudflare Workers and private Cloudflare R2 object storage. Private raw records and report versions are retained without automatic expiry, with archives and analysis through ChatGPT Work and Google Drive. Unacknowledged events may remain in your browser until successfully received; browser blocking or cleared storage can leave gaps. Inquiry identifiers connect observed website activity to requests actually received by Farpoint.

After an accepted form submission, temporary browser session storage may display the confirmation. It is cleared after the confirmation is displayed. A source or campaign code may be included with an inquiry; personal information is not added to page URLs.

Questions and requests

For questions about information you have sent, or to request correction or deletion, email quotes@farpointenergy.com.
