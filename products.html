Sizing basis

Top rating ≥ point-of-interconnection MW ÷ required power factor, plus margin for altitude, ambient, and duty. At 0.95, 45 MW calls for a 50 MVA unit and 90 MW for a 100 MVA unit; a 160 MW site is two 100-class units with phased energization. Storage and hybrid duty profiles are checked before the configuration is confirmed.
