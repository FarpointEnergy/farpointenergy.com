Distribution and medium-voltage transformers

Site-distribution and medium-voltage transformer packages connect facility loads, inverter blocks and storage equipment to the site's electrical system. The transformer configuration follows the equipment duty and installation requirements.

ConfigurationProject choices
Ratings and voltageskVA or MVA rating, voltage ratio and frequency are selected for the connected equipment and operating duty. Multiple unit types and quantities can form one project package.
Installation and terminalsPadmounted and other installation arrangements are reviewed against the site's layout, access and connection requirements.
Insulation and coolingInsulation medium, cooling, temperature rise and sound requirements follow the load profile, site environment and installation specification.
Electrical configurationWinding connections, impedance, taps, losses and grounding are specified for the system. Inverter-associated units also account for the defined harmonic and operating requirements.
Accessories and interfacesProtection, monitoring, terminal arrangements and accessories are selected for the equipment package and its interfaces.
Quantities and delivery phasesUnit quantities and delivery groups can follow the construction sequence, with package identification carried through drawings, testing and shipment.

For data-center site distribution and solar or storage packages, define quantities and delivery phases alongside the electrical requirements.
