Quality

Engineered, inspected, and tested for the duty

Engineering

Engineering review covers short-circuit withstand, thermal duty and insulation coordination against the applicable IEEE C57 or project requirements. Design calculations, including finite-element analysis where required, support the proposed design. Drawings, nameplate and test plan are issued for the specified buyer and utility approvals before the relevant production stages.

Inspection

A stage-gated inspection and test plan with hold, witness and review points is issued for your sign-off before production. For liquid-filled power units, stages include core build, winding, assembly, drying and oil processing. Core steel, conductor and insulation traceability is included in the plan. Your inspector is welcome at the agreed hold and witness points.

Testing

Factory tests verify the ordered transformer against its specification and applicable standard. The test plan defines the required electrical, dielectric and functional checks, supporting design tests, and any additional measurements. Buyer witness points are scheduled with your engineer at order.

Documentation

Test reports, final drawings and the operating and maintenance manual form the digital dossier, kept by serial number. Oil certificates, frequency-response and dissolved-gas baselines, impact-recorder logs and commissioning reports are included where applicable to the equipment and supplied scope.

Inspection and test plan

The stages below illustrate a liquid-filled power-transformer inspection plan. Equipment-specific plans identify the applicable stages and mark each point hold, witness or review.

  • Material receipt: core steel, conductor and insulation traceability
  • Core stacking complete
  • Winding completion, each winding
  • Core-and-coil assembly, before drying
  • Vacuum drying end point: moisture and dew point
  • Tanking and oil filling under vacuum
  • Final assembly and pre-test checks
  • Factory acceptance test — agreed buyer witness points
  • Packing, specified transport monitoring and release for shipment
Site program

Site services and measurements are selected for the equipment, transport condition and agreed commissioning scope.

  • Receiving inspection and any impact-recorder review
  • For gas-shipped units: dew-point and oxygen check before oil filling
  • SFRA compared with the factory baseline where specified
  • Pre-energization tests for the equipment; IEEE C57.152 where applicable
  • DGA at energization and after the first month of service when included in the site program

Interfaces and submittals

Power-transformer interface examples: multi-ratio bushing current transformers, relay accuracy class C800 where specified, neutral CT on grounded-wye windings · control power 125 V DC and 120/240 V AC, 480 V for cooling where required · alarm and trip contacts for temperature, pressure, liquid level and gas · DNP3 or IEC 61850 monitoring options · approval package: outline, nameplate, control schematics, CT curves and bushing drawings as applicable. Distribution-package interfaces are set by the installation and equipment specification.
