Quality and delivery

A clear path from specification to acceptance

The proposal identifies applicable standards, technical exceptions and the planned testing and documentation. The order establishes the approval, inspection and acceptance requirements for your transformer.

QualityDelivery and warrantyFrom specification to delivery

Specification review

Electrical ratings, duty, site conditions and interfaces are reviewed against the buyer's specification. The proposal identifies the offered configuration and any exceptions.

Approval documents

The drawing and document schedule is agreed at order. It can include outlines, nameplates, winding connections, control schematics, terminal details and accessory information.

Inspection and testing

The inspection and test plan defines the applicable tests, acceptance criteria, review points and any buyer witness requirements for the proposed product.

Final documentation

The agreed handover package includes test records, final drawings and operating and maintenance information. Additional records are specified to suit the equipment and project.

For your engineering team

Factory test plan

The applicable product standard and agreed specification determine the test scope, acceptance criteria and witnessing arrangements.

Test areaScope to define
Winding resistance and ratioMeasurements and connection checks appropriate to the windings, taps and specified test standard.
Losses and impedanceNo-load and load measurements, excitation current and impedance at the specified conditions.
Dielectric testsThe applicable applied, induced and impulse test scope is defined by the equipment, voltage class and agreed standard.
Mechanical and functional checksChecks for the supplied tank, terminals, cooling, controls, alarms and accessories, as applicable.
Liquid and insulation recordsOil or other insulating-liquid tests and insulation measurements where required by the equipment and test plan.
Design and additional testsTemperature rise, sound, partial discharge, frequency response and other tests are addressed where applicable or specified; the proposal identifies the test or supporting report scope.
Inspection and release points
  • Material and component documentation
  • Winding and active-part inspection
  • Assembly, drying and filling, as applicable
  • Final assembly and pre-test inspection
  • Factory acceptance tests and buyer witness points
  • Document review and release for shipment
Site activities
  • Receiving inspection and transport-record review
  • Assembly and liquid handling where required
  • Site checks and comparison with factory records
  • Pre-energization testing and commissioning support
Standards and interfaces

Applicable IEEE C57, IEC 60076 or buyer-specified requirements are identified for the proposed product. The specification response records the standards, editions and any exceptions included in the offer.

Specify protection and control interfaces, terminals, monitoring, auxiliary power and accessory requirements with your inquiry. Their scope and approval documents are defined in the proposal.
