Quality

Every unit is proven, not assumed

Where it is built

Farpoint-qualified manufacturing partners operating ISO 9001 quality systems with accredited high-voltage test laboratories. Each partner is qualified against our design standard, process audit, and in-process hold points before it builds a Farpoint unit.

Design authority

Drawings are issued and controlled by Farpoint. Your engineer and interconnecting utility review outline drawings, nameplate, and the test plan before manufacturing releases.

Independent inspection

An independent inspection agency (SGS or Bureau Veritas class) witnesses in-process hold points, the factory acceptance test, and pre-shipment packing on every unit. Customers and owner's engineers are welcome at FAT.

Factory acceptance tests — IEEE C57.12.90

  • Winding resistance · ratio, polarity and phase relation
  • No-load losses and excitation current
  • Load losses and impedance at rated current
  • Applied-voltage and induced-voltage tests, with partial-discharge measurement
  • Lightning impulse test, full and chopped wave
  • Insulation power factor · core ground · leak test
  • Oil dielectric strength, moisture, and dissolved-gas baseline
  • Temperature-rise test on the first unit of each configuration
  • Sound level, when specified

Test reports, oil certificates, and the inspection agency's release are delivered with the unit and archived with its serial number.
