Quality and delivery

Delivery, warranty and service

Delivery is quoted for the project. The proposed schedule includes engineering reviews, production, testing and transport, with the required delivery-to-site date kept distinct from target energization.

A schedule with defined milestones

  1. Order scopeConfirm the configuration, commercial terms, responsibilities and agreed delivery milestones.
  2. EngineeringIssue approval documents and track the review dates needed for the production schedule.
  3. ProductionTrack manufacturing progress and inspection points through the agreed reporting schedule.
  4. Factory acceptanceComplete the agreed tests, review the records and confirm shipment release requirements.
  5. TransportCoordinate packing, transport, site access and receiving responsibilities for the agreed delivery scope.
  6. HandoverProvide the final document package and coordinate any included assembly, testing or commissioning services.

Warranty defined in your proposal

Warranty scope, duration, start date and claim arrangements are included in the proposal and agreed in the order. Storage requirements, site responsibilities and any extended coverage are addressed for the project.

After-sales scope

  • Installation and commissioning support arranged according to project requirements
  • Field testing, diagnostics, liquid processing and repair scope agreed per order
  • Recommended spares and replacement components identified for the supplied equipment
  • After-sales contacts, response arrangements and responsibilities defined in the order
