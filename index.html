Execution

From requirement to delivery

  1. Requirement and proposal

    Send a specification or the project information you have. Farpoint clarifies the requirement and prepares the proposed technical and commercial scope.

  2. Design and approvals

    The order defines the design submissions, review milestones and approval responsibilities before the relevant production stages.

  3. Production and testing

    Progress reporting, inspections and factory acceptance testing follow the agreed order schedule and inspection and test plan.

  4. Delivery and handover

    Shipment, receiving requirements, final documents and any site services are coordinated to the agreed scope.
