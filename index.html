Who we are

The supplier of record

Farpoint Energy supplies main power transformers to developers who cannot wait three years for a substation to be energized. We design to a small set of standard configurations, build them at Farpoint-qualified partner facilities under our own quality program, and stand behind every unit: our contract, our factory acceptance test, our warranty, our service response.

Fixed configurations are the reason we can commit to a delivery month. A repeat design means drawings exist on day one, long-lead materials are reserved before your order is signed, and the factory has built the unit before. Custom is what the 2–4 year market sells. Standard is how we deliver in 12–14 months.

How Farpoint delivers

From single-line diagram to energization

  1. Configuration matchSend your single-line diagram and site data. We confirm the standard configuration that fits and any project-specific values within the design window.
  2. Firm quote, committed monthPrice, delivery month, and terms — binding — within ten business days.
  3. Drawings for approvalOutline, nameplate, and test plan issued to your engineer and interconnecting utility. The approval cycle is built into the schedule.
  4. Build and witnessed testProduction in a reserved slot. Full routine suite to IEEE C57.12.90, witnessed by an independent inspection agency. You are welcome at FAT.
  5. Delivery and energizationExport packing with impact recorders, ocean and inland transport, DAP to your site. Commissioning supervision at energization. US-based service response under warranty.
