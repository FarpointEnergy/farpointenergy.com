Process

How it works

  1. Send what you haveA configuration from the catalog, your specification, or a single-line diagram and site data. We return a quotation with a compliance matrix.
  2. Drawings for approvalOutline, nameplate, and test plan issued to your engineer and interconnecting utility; long-lead materials committed at order.
  3. Manufacture and witnessed testThe full IEEE C57.12.90 routine program on every unit. Witnessed FAT is standard.
  4. Delivery and energizationTransport to site, assembly, oil filling, site tests, and commissioning. Warranty and field service from Farpoint.
Warranty & service

Three years, from energization

Every unit ships with a Farpoint warranty of 36 months from energization, with the unit stored to Farpoint's storage procedure before energization, covering materials, workmanship, and design. Repair or replacement is at Farpoint's cost, including transport and site labor. The warranty is assignable to your lender or a successor owner. Coverage extends to five years from energization as an option.
